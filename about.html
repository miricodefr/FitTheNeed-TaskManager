About FitTheNeed

FitTheNeed (FTN) is a task management platform with a soon to come AI website builder. FTN helps teams track projects, deadlines, and priorities in one place.

Fast Planning

Create tasks quickly, set due dates, and break website builds into steps.

Clear Priorities

Assign High/Medium/Low priority and filter tasks to focus on what matters.

Analytics

Visualize progress with charts for completed vs pending tasks and priority distribution.
